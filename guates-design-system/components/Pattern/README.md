# Pattern

One of the three brand patterns, filling its box, with every shape bound to a palette token.

**Names.** `cruces` (woven horizontal and vertical bands), `formas` (organic discs and ellipses on crema), `franjas` (rows of alternating strong and light stripes). The list is `Guates.patterns`.

**Props.** `name`, `width` (default `100%`), `height` (default 240), `fit` (`slice` crops to cover, the default; `meet` shows the whole artboard).

**Use** for wrapping paper, box interiors, section dividers, social backgrounds and page edges, full-bleed or as an accent band. Patterns are colour fields, not containers: set text on a ColorBlock or crema panel above them (as the brandbook's introduction sits on `cruces`), and keep the logo's protection area clear of the pattern.

**Don't** recolour individual stripes off-palette, rotate a pattern, or run two patterns in one composition.
